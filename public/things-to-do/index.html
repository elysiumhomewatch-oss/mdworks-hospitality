Things To Do

The KwaZulu-Natal South Coast at your doorstep

The stretch of coastline around Pennington and Umdoni Point is one of South Africa's best-kept secrets. Whether you're chasing waves, wildlife, golf or simply quiet, you'll find it within minutes of Perry's.

Golf

A Golfer's Stretch of Coast

The South Coast is famous among golfers for its combination of spectacular scenery and world-class courses. Perry's is within easy reach of four excellent venues:

  • ✦ Umdoni Park Golf Club — a few minutes away, one of the finest on the coast
  • ✦ Selborne Golf Estate — championship course in Bot River Valley
  • ✦ Penn Valley Golf Club — relaxed club in Pennington town
  • ✦ More courses within 30 minutes up and down the coast

Ask Debbi about golf-and-stay packages.

Wildlife

Whales, Dolphins & Marine Life

The South Coast is one of the best places in South Africa to watch humpback whales on their annual migration (June to November). Dolphins are resident year-round and can often be spotted surfing the breaks right from the beach.

For divers, the South Coast's warm Indian Ocean waters offer rich reefs, bull sharks, Zambezi rays and leatherback turtles. Aliwal Shoal, an hour up the coast, is ranked among the world's top dive sites.

Also nearby

More to Explore

Scottburgh

The nearest town — great restaurants, the famous Scottburgh Slides waterpark, and a safe swimming beach.

Hiking & Walking

Coastal forest trails start at Umdoni Point itself. The cliffside paths offer stunning views over the Indian Ocean.

Fishing

Rock and surf fishing from the coastline around Pennington, or charter a boat for deep-sea fishing.

iSimangaliso Wetland Park

A UNESCO World Heritage Site — crocodiles, hippos, tropical beaches and snorkelling reefs within a day trip.

Local Markets

Weekend craft and produce markets along the South Coast showcase local artisans and fresh South African flavours.

Surfing

The South Coast has consistent surf breaks suitable for beginners and experienced surfers alike.
