Section 1

Getting Started — Logging In

Your admin panel is a private, password-protected area of the website that only you can access. Guests never see it.

How to open it

  1. Open any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox — all work fine).
  2. Go to: perrys.pages.dev/admin/
  3. Type your password when prompted and press Enter or click Login.
💡Bookmark the admin page in your browser so you can get back to it in one click.

Your password

MD Works set your password when the site was built and sent it to you separately. If you've forgotten it or want it changed, contact MD Works — it takes less than a minute to reset.

⚠️Do not share your password with guests. The admin panel lets you delete bookings and change the site — keep it private.

Logging out

Simply close the browser tab. The session ends automatically after a period of inactivity.

Section 2

Dashboard — Your Overview

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after logging in. It gives you a quick snapshot of what's happening without needing to click around.

What you'll see

  • Booking stats — total bookings, how many are pending (waiting for your confirmation), confirmed and cancelled.
  • Recent bookings — the last few enquiries at a glance, with guest name, dates and status.
  • Outstanding items checklist — a preview of your Pending Items list (see Section 12), reminding you what's still to do before opening.

To explore any area in detail, click the relevant item in the left-hand menu.

Section 3

Managing Bookings

When a guest fills in the booking enquiry form on the website, their details are automatically saved and you receive a WhatsApp message with all the information. You then confirm or decline the booking manually and update the status in the admin panel.

The booking flow

  1. Guest fills in the booking form on the website.
  2. You receive a WhatsApp message with their full details.
  3. You reply to the guest via WhatsApp to confirm or discuss availability.
  4. Once confirmed, log into admin, find the booking and mark it Confirmed.

Viewing all bookings

Click All Bookings in the left menu. You'll see a table of all enquiries, newest first. You can filter by status — Pending, Confirmed or Cancelled — using the buttons at the top.

Opening a booking

Click any row in the table to open the full booking detail panel on the right. This shows you the guest's name, contact details, room preference, dates, number of guests, any special requests, and when they submitted the enquiry.

Updating a booking status

ButtonWhat it does
ConfirmMarks the booking as confirmed. The status badge turns green.
CancelMarks it as cancelled. The badge turns grey. The record stays in the system.
DeletePermanently removes the booking record. Use this for test entries or obvious spam — not for real bookings you might need later.
⚠️Delete is permanent. Once deleted, a booking cannot be recovered. If you're unsure, mark it Cancelled instead — it stays on record but is clearly closed.
💡The admin panel is your record-keeping tool. The actual confirmation still happens on WhatsApp — keep that conversation as your backup.
Section 4

Site Settings

This is where you keep your contact details and social media links up to date. These appear automatically across the whole website — in the footer, on the contact page, and in the WhatsApp booking messages guests receive.

Site Logo

At the top of Site Settings, you can upload your logo. When a logo is uploaded, it replaces the text "Perry's" in the navigation bar on every page of the site. If you haven't uploaded one yet, the text shows instead — both work perfectly.

FieldWhat to put here
LogoYour logo file. PNG with a transparent background works best. Recommended size: 400×160 pixels. Maximum 500 KB. See Section 13 for image guidance.
💡To upload a logo: click the upload zone, choose your file, and wait for the green success message. To remove it, click the Remove logo button. The nav text returns immediately.

Contact details

FieldWhat to put here
PhoneYour main phone number, e.g. +27 39 978 0000. Include the country code.
WhatsAppThe number guests message to confirm bookings. This is the number that receives the automatic booking notification. You can use a local format (e.g. 083 123 4567) — the site converts it automatically.
EmailYour contact email address, e.g. hello@perrys.co.za
Full AddressYour street address as you'd like it to appear on the Contact page.

Social media

FieldWhat to put here
Instagram URLYour full Instagram page link, e.g. https://instagram.com/perrysatudmonipoint
Facebook URLYour full Facebook page link, e.g. https://facebook.com/perrysatudmonipoint

Social links are hidden on the site until you fill them in. Once added, icons appear in the footer automatically. Leave blank if you're not ready.

Google Maps embed

This adds an interactive map to the Contact page. To get the embed code:

  1. Go to maps.google.com and search for your property.
  2. Click Share, then Embed a map.
  3. Copy the full code shown (it starts with <iframe).
  4. Paste it into the Google Maps Embed field in admin.
  5. Click Save Settings.
💡Always click Save Settings at the bottom after making any changes in this section. Changes are not saved automatically.
Section 5

Specials Banner

The Specials Banner is a strip that appears at the very top of every page of the site — above the navigation bar. Use it to announce a promotion, an opening special, a seasonal offer, or anything time-sensitive you want every visitor to see the moment they land on the site.

💡The banner is off by default. Nothing shows on the site until you turn it on.
FieldWhat to put here
Active (toggle)Switch this on to show the banner. Switch it off to hide it. The content is saved even when the banner is off, so you can prepare it in advance and turn it on when you're ready.
Banner TitleThe bold text that leads the banner, e.g. Opening Special — September 2026
Banner Body TextA short description of the offer, e.g. Stay 2 nights and get your third night at half price.
CTA LabelThe text on the link button, e.g. View Rates or Book Now
CTA LinkWhere the button takes guests when they click it. Use /rates/ for the rates page, /contact/ for the contact page, etc.

Click Save Banner when done. Toggle the Active switch and save again to turn it on or off at any time.

Section 6

Our Story

This section controls the main story text on the Our Story page — the history of the Barker Farmhouse and Perry's. It's separate from all the other page content editors because it's your main brand story and deserves its own space.

FieldWhat to put here
HeadlineThe heading that appears above the story text, e.g. A Historic South Coast Retreat
Body TextYour full story. Write it here just as you would in a Word document. To create a new paragraph, press Enter twice (leave a blank line between paragraphs). The site will format it automatically.
💡Keep paragraphs to 3–5 sentences for readability on screen. Short paragraphs are easier to read than long blocks of text.

Click Save Story when done.

Section 7

💡Each tab has its own Save button at the bottom. Make sure you click the right one after editing. Changes on the Home tab, for example, won't be saved if you click the Rooms Save button.

Living & Dining section (Rooms page)

This is a hidden section that can be switched on when you have content and a photo ready for it. It lets you tell guests about the shared living and dining spaces in the farmhouse.

  1. Fill in the Heading and Body text for the section.
  2. Upload a photo using the upload zone (recommended size: 1200×900 pixels, landscape).

Section 8

Rooms

The Rooms section lets you edit the five individual room cards that appear on the Rooms page and the Home page preview. Each room has its own set of fields.

Room fields

FieldWhat to put here
NameThe name of the room, e.g. The Garden Room or The Farmhouse Suite
Bed Typee.g. King, Queen, Twin, or King + Single
Occupancye.g. 2 adults, or 2 adults + 1 child
DescriptionA short paragraph about the room — its character, views, feel. 2–4 sentences is ideal.
AmenitiesA comma-separated list of what's in the room, e.g. En-suite, AC, Smart TV, Safe, Tea station
PlaceholderWhen this is ticked, the room card shows an "Enquire" button instead of "Book this room", and the card is slightly muted. Use this for rooms whose details aren't finalised yet. Untick it when the room is ready.

💡Unlike other sections, the Rooms editor saves all five rooms at once. Fill in as many as you're ready for, then click Save All Rooms at the bottom.

Uploading a room photo

  1. Scroll to the room you want to update.
  2. Click the Upload Room Image zone (or anywhere in the dashed box).
  3. Choose your photo from your computer or phone.
  4. Wait for the preview to appear — this confirms the upload was successful.
  5. Click Save All Rooms to apply the change to the live site.
Section 9

Rates & Laundry

This section controls the rates table on the Rates & Book page and the laundry price list on the Experience page.

Accommodation rates

Each row in the rates table is one package (e.g. Bed & Breakfast, Dinner Bed & Breakfast). For each package you set three prices:

ColumnWhat it means
PackageThe name of the package as it appears in the table header.
Standard RateThe price per person per night on weeknights (Sunday–Thursday).
Weekend RateThe price per person per night on Friday and Saturday nights.
Peak RateThe price per person per night during peak season (school holidays, December, Easter).

You can add a new package by clicking + Add Package, and remove one by clicking the bin icon on the right.

Peak Season Note

A short note below the rates table where you can explain what "peak season" means for your property, e.g. Peak season applies to school holidays, long weekends and 15 Dec – 10 Jan.

Laundry prices

Works the same way as the rates table — each row is one laundry item (e.g. Shirt, Trousers, Dress) with a price. Add rows with + Add Item and remove them with the bin icon.

💡Click Save Rates at the bottom after any changes. Rates and laundry prices are saved together with one button.
Section 10

Things To Do

This controls the cards on the Things To Do page — local activities, attractions and experiences near Perry's that you want to recommend to guests.

Each card has a Title (e.g. Pennington Beaches) and a Description (2–4 sentences about what it is and why guests will enjoy it).

Adding a new card

  1. Click + Add Item at the top of the Things To Do editor.
  2. A new empty card appears at the bottom.
  3. Fill in the Title and Description.
  4. Click Save Things To Do.

Removing a card

Click the Remove button on the card you want to delete, then click Save Things To Do to confirm.

💡Cards appear on the page in the order you have them in the editor. Drag to reorder (or ask MD Works to add reordering if you'd like that feature).

Named images

💡Each named image upload zone shows a preview of the current photo. If no photo has been uploaded yet, the site falls back to a default placeholder. Upload your own photos as soon as you have them.

Gallery images

The gallery is the photo grid on the Gallery page. You can upload as many photos as you like. Each photo can be tagged with a category (Rooms, Exterior, Dining, Surroundings) so guests can filter the gallery.

Uploading a gallery photo

  1. Scroll to the Gallery Images card.
  2. Select a category from the dropdown (or leave it as General).
  3. Click the upload zone and choose your photo.
  4. The photo uploads automatically and appears in the gallery immediately — no save button needed for gallery uploads.

Removing a gallery photo

Click the Remove button below the photo you want to delete. It's removed from the gallery immediately.

⚠️Removing a gallery photo is permanent. It is deleted from the image server and cannot be recovered. Keep your original photos backed up on your computer or phone.

See Section 13 for recommended photo sizes and quality guidance.

Section 12

Pending Items Checklist

The Pending Items section is a private checklist — only visible in the admin panel, never on the public website. It tracks the outstanding items that need to be confirmed before Perry's is fully ready to launch.

Each item has a label (the task) and a done checkbox. Tick the checkbox when an item is complete. MD Works can add or remove items from the list as things get done — just let us know.

The checklist also appears as a summary on your Dashboard so you always know at a glance what's still outstanding.

💡Click Save Checklist after ticking any items, or your progress won't be saved.
Section 13

Image Size Reference

Good photos make a huge difference to how your site looks and feels. Here's a quick reference for what works best in each spot. You don't need to get these exact — close is fine — but staying roughly within these guidelines will keep the site looking sharp.

Format guide

  • JPG or WebP — best for all photographs. Keep file size under 1 MB per photo.
  • PNG — best when you need a transparent background (e.g. your logo). Keep under 500 KB.
  • SVG — ideal for logos if your designer can supply one. Very small file size, crisp at any size.
ImageRecommended sizeFormatNotes
Logo400 × 160 pxPNG (transparent) or SVGWide format works best in the nav bar. Keep it horizontal.
Hero (homepage)1920 × 1080 pxJPG or WebPThis is your biggest, most important photo — use your very best shot of the property.
OG / Share image1200 × 630 pxJPG or WebPWide landscape format. Should look great as a thumbnail.
Named images (farmhouse, dining, etc.)1200 × 900 pxJPG or WebP4:3 ratio (landscape). These appear in content sections alongside text.
Room images1200 × 900 pxJPG or WebP4:3 ratio. Shoot from the corner of the room to show as much as possible.
Gallery images1200 × 900 px or 900 × 1200 pxJPG or WebPBoth landscape and portrait work in the gallery grid.
Living & Dining section1200 × 900 pxJPG or WebP4:3 landscape, showing the shared lounge or dining area.
💡If your photos are too large (over 2–3 MB each), the site will still work but may load slowly on mobile. You can use a free tool like squoosh.app to resize and compress photos before uploading.
Section 14

Tips & Common Questions

I made a change and don't see it on the site

Most changes appear within a few seconds of saving. If you don't see the update, try a hard refresh in your browser: hold Ctrl and press F5 on Windows, or hold ⌘ Cmd + Shift and press R on a Mac. This forces the browser to reload fresh content.

I clicked Save but nothing happened

Check for a red error message just below the section title. If one appears, read it — it usually explains what went wrong. If you're not sure, take a screenshot and send it to MD Works.

Do I need to tell MD Works every time I make a change?

No — the admin panel is designed for you to use independently. Make changes whenever you like. The only time you need MD Works is if something isn't working, or if you want to add a feature that isn't already there.

Can guests see what I'm doing in the admin panel?

No. The admin panel is completely separate from the public website. Guests cannot access it and will never know it exists.

What happens if I accidentally delete something?

For text content (headings, paragraphs, rates), you can retype what was there and save again. For images that have been deleted from the gallery, they are gone permanently — which is why we recommend keeping your original photos backed up. For bookings, deleted records cannot be recovered, but your WhatsApp conversation is always there as a backup.

Can I edit the site on my phone?

Yes. The admin panel works on mobile browsers, though it's more comfortable on a tablet or desktop. For uploading photos, your phone is actually the easiest option — you can take a photo and upload it directly from the camera roll.

Something looks broken — what do I do?

Take a screenshot showing the issue, note which page or admin section it's on, and send it to MD Works via WhatsApp or email. Most issues can be fixed remotely within a few hours.
