Privacy & POPIA

How we collect, use and protect your personal information

Last updated: September 2026. This policy applies to Perry's @ Umdoni Point (the "Guesthouse"), operating from Pennington, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. We are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who We Are

Perry's @ Umdoni Point is a boutique guesthouse situated at Umdoni Point, Pennington, KwaZulu-Natal. We are the responsible party for the personal information you provide to us through this website, our booking form, WhatsApp, email or any other means of contact.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect only the personal information necessary to process your booking and communicate with you. This includes:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number / WhatsApp number
  • Dates of stay and number of guests
  • Room and package preferences
  • Special requests or requirements you choose to share

We do not collect payment card numbers, ID numbers, passport numbers or other sensitive identification through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Process and confirm your booking enquiry
  • Communicate with you before, during and after your stay
  • Send you information relevant to your booking (arrival details, confirmations, etc.)
  • Comply with any legal or regulatory obligations

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Where Your Information Is Stored

Booking enquiry data submitted through this website is stored in Cloudflare's KV (Key-Value) storage, which is processed in data centres within the European Economic Area and the United States. Cloudflare is GDPR-compliant. Your information may be accessed by the guesthouse owner for the purposes described above.

5. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain booking records for a period of three (3) years from your date of stay, or as required by South African law, whichever is longer. After this period your records are deleted.

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to the processing of your information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly via the details on our Contact page.

7. Cookies and Analytics

This website does not currently use cookies for tracking or analytics. If this changes we will update this policy and notify visitors accordingly.

8. WhatsApp Communications

When you contact us via WhatsApp or when we send you a WhatsApp message, your message content and phone number are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy (Meta Platforms, Inc.). We use WhatsApp solely for guest communication and booking confirmation — not for marketing.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Our website is served over HTTPS and booking data is stored in secure cloud infrastructure.

10. Information Regulator

If you believe your rights under POPIA have been violated and we have not resolved your concern, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa:

  • Website: www.justice.gov.za/inforeg/
  • Email: inforeg@justice.gov.za

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page. Material changes will be communicated to recent guests by email where appropriate.

12. Contact

For any privacy-related queries, please contact us via our Contact page.
