The Chef

Kayla Ann's Table

Kayla Ann has spent decades honing her craft in this very kitchen. As founder of the former chef school on the property, she brings decades of culinary passion to Perry's dining experience.

Each evening she prepares a fresh two-course set dinner for guests — hearty, seasonal, and entirely her own. It's the kind of meal that makes you linger at the table long after the plates are cleared.

Dinner is available as part of select packages. See our rates page for details.
