Booking Terms & Conditions

Please read before confirming your reservation

Last updated: September 2026. These terms apply to all bookings at Perry's @ Umdoni Point. By confirming a reservation you agree to these terms. Some details (cancellation policy, payment terms, children and pet policies) are still being finalised — we will confirm current terms when you book.

1. Reservations

A booking enquiry submitted through our website, WhatsApp or email constitutes a request for accommodation — not a confirmed reservation. Your booking is confirmed only once you have received written confirmation from Perry's @ Umdoni Point and any required deposit has been received.

2. Check-In and Check-Out

  • Check-in: From 14:00
  • Check-out: By 10:00

Early check-in and late check-out may be arranged on request, subject to availability and at the discretion of management. Additional charges may apply.

3. Rates and Packages

All rates are quoted per person per night in South African Rand (ZAR) and are inclusive of VAT where applicable. Rates are subject to change without notice until a confirmed reservation is in place. The rate applicable to your booking is that confirmed in writing at the time of reservation.

Breakfast and dinner packages are as described on our rates page. Dinner packages require a minimum of 2 persons and must be booked in advance — late dinner requests cannot always be accommodated.

4. Payment

Payment terms are currently being finalised. Full details will be confirmed at the time of booking. We anticipate requiring a deposit to secure your reservation, with the balance due on arrival or as otherwise agreed in writing.

5. Cancellation Policy

Our cancellation policy is currently being finalised. Full terms will be confirmed at the time of booking. We recommend that guests obtain travel insurance to protect against unforeseen cancellations.

6. No-Shows

If you fail to arrive on your confirmed check-in date without prior notice, the full cost of your stay may be charged. Please notify us as early as possible if your plans change.

7. House Rules

  • Smoking: Smoking is not permitted inside the guesthouse. A designated outdoor smoking area is available.
  • Quiet hours: We ask all guests to observe quiet hours between 22:00 and 07:00.
  • Visitors: Unregistered visitors are not permitted to stay overnight without prior arrangement.
  • Parties and events: The guesthouse may not be used for events or gatherings without prior written consent from management.
  • Damage: Guests are liable for any damage caused to the property, fixtures or fittings during their stay.

8. Children

Our children policy is currently being finalised. Please contact us directly for current terms.

9. Pets

Access and any applicable fees or discounts will be confirmed at check-in. Perry's @ Umdoni Point is not responsible for the operation, availability or safety standards of third-party facilities on the estate.

Perry's @ Umdoni Point and its staff accept no liability for loss, damage, theft, injury or illness to guests or their property during their stay, except where such liability cannot be excluded under South African law. Guests use all facilities at their own risk.
